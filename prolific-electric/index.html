Questions

Good to Know

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. Call, text or send the form with a few details and photos, and we'll get back to you with a price.

Do you do homes and businesses?

Both. We work in homes and golf communities, plus businesses like gas stations, shops and salons.

Can you install my EV charger?

Yes. We install Level 2 home chargers on their own dedicated circuit, like the 50-amp charger we put in for a BMW i4.

What areas do you cover?

The Desert Cities: Coachella, Indio, La Quinta, Palm Desert, Rancho Mirage, Cathedral City and nearby. Not sure? Just call.

I bought my own light fixture. Can you install it?

Usually, yes. Send us a photo or link of the fixture and where it's going, and we'll let you know.
